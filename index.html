Online & Offline Admission Procedure

Follow these 4 simple steps to secure your seat at ASTC

Go to Online Admission Form →
1
Check Eligibility

Verify minimum qualifications: 10+2 MPC for B.Tech, relevant B.Tech for M.Tech, and SSC for Diploma courses.

2
Entrance & Counseling

Qualify AP EAMCET / ECET / POLYCET. Choose ASTC (Garividi) during web counseling for Convener Quota.

3
Document Verification

Submit allotment letter, original mark sheets, transfer certificates, and caste/income certificates (if applicable).

4
Enrollment & Induction

Complete fee formalities, collect your student ID & DELNET library card, and join the orientation week.
